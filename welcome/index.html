Nichely is ready

It works on Amazon.com search pages, in Books and Kindle Store. Three steps:

  1. Search a keyword on Amazon.com, for example gratitude journal for teens.
  2. Click the green Check niche button at the bottom right of the page.
  3. Read the score and the reasons, then open Keyword ideas to find a better angle.

Try it now with one of these:

Books: gratitude journal for teens Kindle: cozy mystery cat

Good to know

  • Sales are estimates from the Best Sellers Rank. The rank moves every hour, so check a niche on two or three days before you commit.
  • Your last checks stay in the panel, so you can compare keywords side by side.
  • Pin Nichely to the toolbar (the puzzle icon, then the pin) to search Amazon from any tab.
